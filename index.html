How the Smart Warehouse Monitor Works

This system continuously safeguards fresh produce by pairing automated wall sensors with smart cameras, real-time cooling triggers, and immediate WhatsApp mobile notifications.

1
Smart automated cooling: If the warehouse rises above your cooling limit, the 3 wall coolers turn on automatically and stay active until temperature is 2°C below your limit (this prevents wasteful rapid switching).
2
Vegetable protection: Sensitive gas sensors on each carton detect spoiled produce right away so you can remove it before other cartons are affected.
3
Instant photo proof for spills: When liquid is spilled on the floor, the nearest corner camera snaps a photo with highlighted boundaries and sends it to WhatsApp so staff can clean it immediately.
4
Never miss an emergency: Critical alerts are delivered straight to the owner's WhatsApp and saved in the permanent dashboard event log.
